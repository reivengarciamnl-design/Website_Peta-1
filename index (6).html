About Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that allows computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

What is AI?

AI is a technology that allows computers to perform tasks such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, and solving problems.

How Does AI Work?

Many AI systems use data to recognize patterns and produce answers, predictions, or recommendations.

AI in Everyday Life

AI is used in navigation apps, voice assistants, translation tools, and online recommendations.

Why is AI Important?

AI can help people learn new things, explore ideas, and complete certain tasks more efficiently.
