Learn About AI

Understand the benefits, challenges, and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.

Benefits of AI

AI can help people in many ways.

  • Helps explain difficult lessons.
  • Provides ideas and suggestions.
  • Helps organize information.
  • Supports creativity and productivity.

Challenges of AI

AI also has limitations and risks.

  • It can provide incorrect information.
  • It may reflect bias.
  • It can raise privacy concerns.
  • It should not replace human judgment.

Responsible Use of AI

AI should be used carefully and responsibly. Users should understand its limitations and make their own decisions.

  • Check information using reliable sources.
  • Protect personal and private information.
  • Follow your teacher's rules for schoolwork.
  • Use AI to understand lessons, not just copy answers.
  • Think critically about AI-generated content.
