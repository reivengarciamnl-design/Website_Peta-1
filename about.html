About Artificial Intelligence

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that allows computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

How Does AI Work?

AI learns from data and uses patterns to generate responses, make predictions, or perform tasks.

AI in Everyday Life

AI can be found in smartphones, social media, search engines, recommendation systems, games, and many other technologies.

Why is AI Important?

AI can help people solve problems, learn new things, save time, and make many tasks easier.
